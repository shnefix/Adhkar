## Adhkar 3.1

### Updates
- From this version on, Adhkar tells you when a new version is out, with **Install** and **Later**. Install
  downloads it from GitHub with a progress bar and opens Android's installer; Later asks again a day later.
- Settings › Updates: turn the check off, or check now. It is the app's only use of the internet: when you
  open the app, at most twice a day, it asks GitHub for the latest version. Nothing about you is sent.

### Widgets
- Widgets no longer come out squashed. Some launchers (ColorOS, for example) report a size that isn't the
  space they give the widget; now the card always fills that space and the picture keeps its own shape.
- The azkar-times dials show the time with a live clock, so it is always right (it could be up to 15
  minutes behind).
- In Arabic, the large beads widget's undo button, the classic large widgets' +1 and Reset buttons and the
  counters grid now respond where they are drawn.
- Taps on the margins around a widget's picture count too.

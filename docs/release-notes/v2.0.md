## Adhkar 2.0

### New azkar
- 26 new built-in folders, 644 azkar: prayer, tasabih, comprehensive duas, Quranic duas, duas of the
  prophets, adhan, wudu, home, food, hajj and umrah, completing the Quran, the 99 names of Allah, duas for
  the deceased, travel, distress, istikhara, the sick, rain and wind, clothes, gatherings, anger, the
  market, and the ruqyah. They start in the Library; pin the ones you want to Home from Customize.
- The first Home folder is now أذكار قصيرة, with three more azkar: سبحان الله وبحمده،
  اللهم إنك عفو تحب العفو فاعف عني، and يا حي يا قيوم برحمتك أستغيث.
- A new folder, إذا مُدِحت, with the dua for when people praise you.
- Clearer names for some folders (for example الاستغفار, أذكار بعد الصلاة, مختارات).

### Sources
- A book button on the reader card turns it over to show where the zikr comes from: the ayah, or the
  hadith text from the six books, with its reference and grade. Azkar with no source have no button.
- Hide sources for one zikr (the book button on its row in Edit folder), for a folder (Edit folder), or
  for the whole app (Settings).

### Reading
- Zikr titles above, below, both or hidden; a progress slider you can drag, or the plain bar.
- Finishing a folder takes you back to where you opened it (Home, Library or My azkar).

### Settings and widgets
- Settings (gear) and Reminders (bell) are separate pages.
- The two strip widgets are drawn as proper 4×1 bars; the Today widget no longer shows the date; on the
  morning and evening folders, tapping the progress strip moves to the next zikr.

### Fixes
- A crash when the reader closed while a card was still moving.

Your azkar, folders and settings are kept when you update. As always, no internet permission, no ads.

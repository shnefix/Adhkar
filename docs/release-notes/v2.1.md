## Adhkar 2.1

### Sources
- A new source page, in two styles to choose from in Settings › صفحة المصدر:
  - **Tabs on the card**: the zikr in a framed card, then tabs for the hadith, its chain of narrators
    (السند) and its takhrij (التخريج).
  - **Sheet over the reader**: the source slides up over the zikr you are reading, with the hadith, its
    chain, and buttons to copy the hadith or go back.
- The chain of narrators runs from the Prophet ﷺ to the book's author, with who each one is (صحابي،
  تابعي، شيخ البخاري …). It is only shown when the hadith text has a clear chain.
- Many more azkar now show the full hadith text and its grade.
- The reference no longer shows twice on the source page.

### Reading
- A new counting circle: a dashed gold ring with the count inside, smaller, in the card's bottom-right
  corner. Tapping it counts and never moves on.
- Focus mode keeps the count visible; tapping the card counts, and swiping moves to another zikr.

### Fixes
- An import no longer stops half way, or crashes, if you leave the screen while it runs.
- Two reminders can no longer replace each other's notification.
- A backup file can no longer move the morning, evening or before-sleep reminder away from its prayer.

## Adhkar 2.1

### Sources
- A new source page, in two styles to choose from in Settings › صفحة المصدر:
  - **Tabs on the card**: the zikr in a framed card, then tabs for the hadith, its chain of narrators
    (السند) and its takhrij (التخريج).
  - **Sheet over the reader**: the source slides up over the zikr you are reading, with the hadith, its
    chain, and buttons to copy the hadith or go back.
- The chain of narrators runs from the Prophet ﷺ to the book's author, with who each one is (صحابي،
  تابعي، شيخ البخاري …). It is only shown when the hadith text has a clear chain.
- Many more azkar now show the full hadith text and its grade.
- The reference no longer shows twice on the source page.

### Reading
- A new counting circle: a dashed gold ring with the count inside, smaller, in the card's bottom-right
  corner. Tapping it counts and never moves on.
- Focus mode keeps the count visible; tapping the card counts, and swiping moves to another zikr.

### Fixes
- An import no longer stops half way, or crashes, if you leave the screen while it runs.
- Two reminders can no longer replace each other's notification.
- A backup file can no longer move the morning, evening or before-sleep reminder away from its prayer.

### Home
- A new Home: the first folder in your order is a big card beside two small ones, then a wide card and
  pairs. Every card shows how many azkar it holds, in softer text.
- Two looks, chosen in Customize › Home: **Colorful** (default, a tint and icon colour per collection) or
  **Plain**.
- The completion screen and its setting are gone; finishing a collection just closes the reader.

### Widgets
- A redesigned Widgets screen with a live preview on a home-screen strip drawn to scale, thumbnails of the
  widgets you placed, and an Add tile.
- A new Add widget sheet with size filters, real previews and a confirm bar.
- Faster taps: the tapped widget redraws first. Azkar-times dials open Tasabih outside the morning and
  evening windows. The sunrise and moon widgets are merged.
- Dhikr strip, progress strip and Today are retired; widgets you had of them stay off. The share button is
  removed from the tahlil and morning & evening widgets.
- Widget, reminder and Add widget folder pickers show the folder's path and only list folders with azkar.

### Library
- Search across azkar, folders and sources. Nested folders and built-in groups.
- Cleaner texts, grades filled in Arabic, weak and ungraded azkar hidden (the eye button shows them).
  Istighfar comes first in التسابيح. Four folders were removed (الاستغفار، أذكار خفيفة، الغسل، التشهد) and
  the short azkar folder was dropped.
- Backups keep hidden azkar, the Home order.

### Reader
- A new card background for the OLED theme, with brighter Arabic text.

### More fixes
- Tracking ignores folders with nothing to read, so an empty or parent folder no longer holds a streak at 0.
- The reader only credits azkar read in order; jumping with the slider no longer marks the rest as read.
- A widget tap redraws every widget on that folder. Renaming a folder saves after a pause in typing.

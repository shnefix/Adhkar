Date: 2026-11-10.

## How this was checked (read first)
- I searched the web (dorar.net, islamqa.info, binbaz.org.sa, sunnah.com, hadith aggregators). **sunnah.com and dorar.net block direct page fetches (HTTP 403)**, so I relied on search-result summaries of those sites, not on reading the full pages.
- Items marked **(web)** were confirmed by those searches. Items marked **(memory)** are what I know of the hadith literature and were **not re-checked online**. Please treat those as less certain.
- This is a research aid, not a fatwa. Where scholars differ (Albani vs. others) both views are noted.

Legend: ✅ authentic and specifically a morning/evening dhikr · ⚠️ authentic text but **not specifically** a morning/evening dhikr (or only one of the two) · ❌ the morning/evening chain is weak.

## ⚠️ Not specifically morning/evening (candidates to move out)

| Card | Finding |
|---|---|
| **أستغفر الله وأتوب إليه ×100** (both folders; cited Bukhari 6307) | **(web)** Authentic as a daily dhikr, but it is not a fixed morning/evening dhikr. The reports say the Prophet ﷺ sought forgiveness over 70 / 100 times *a day*. |
| **لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك له ×100** (both folders; Abu Dawud 5077 + Bukhari 3293) | **(memory)** The morning/evening version (Abu Dawud 5077) is **×10**; the ×100 is a separate "any time during the day" reward (Bukhari 3293). The ×100 card mixes the two. |
| **أعوذ بكلمات الله التامات من شر ما خلق ×3** | **(web)** Authentic (Tirmidhi, hasan; Albani hasan) but the hadith says **in the evening** ("من قال حين يمسي") so it is an evening dhikr. Some narrations of Tabarani add morning, but it is weak. The **morning folder** copy rests on this weaker point; the app's cited Muslim 2708/2709 is the *"when you stop at a place"* dua, a different context. |
| **اللهم إني أعوذ بك من الهم والحزن… ×3** (cited Abu Dawud 1555) | **(web)** The Bukhari/Muslim wording (Anas) is authentic but general. The morning/evening wording (Abu Said, Abu Dawud 1555) has Ghassan ibn Awf and is weakened (Albani: da'if; Mundhiri: not below hasan). |
| **أستغفر الله العظيم الذي لا إله إلا هو الحي القيوم وأتوب إليه ×3** (Abu Dawud 1517) | **(memory)** Authentic, but it is the general istighfar (after wrong-doing/prayer), not tied to morning or evening. |
| **يا رب لك الحمد كما ينبغي لجلال وجهك ×3** (Ibn Majah 3801) | **(memory)** A general dua of praise, with a weak chain; not a morning/evening dhikr. |
| **اللهم إنا نعوذ بك من أن نشرك بك شيئًا نعلمه… ×3** (Ahmad 19606) | **(web)** Authentic (Mundhiri: chain acceptable; Albani sahih for the Abu Bakr wording) but the search did **not** confirm that it is a morning/evening dhikr. It is a general protection dua against hidden shirk. |

## ❌ Weak chain (morning/evening)

| Card | Finding |
|---|---|
| **رضيت بالله ربًا وبالإسلام دينًا وبمحمد نبيًا ×3** (app cites **Bukhari 93**) | **(web)** The morning/evening report is **Abu Dawud 5072** (and Ahmad), wording "رسولًا", "كان حقًا على الله أن يرضيه"; Albani graded it **weak**, others (Zubair Ali Zai) hasan. The "وجبت له الجنة / نبيًا" wording is Muslim 1884 (Abu Said), which is **not** morning/evening. **The app's citation (Bukhari 93) looks wrong** and should be corrected. |
| **اللهم ما أصبح بي من نعمة… (أمسى)** (Abu Dawud 5073) | **(web)** Albani: **weak** (Da'if al-Jami' 5730, Da'if al-Targhib 385, Al-Kalim al-Tayyib). Narrator Abdullah ibn Anbasah is unknown. The meaning is fine; the chain is not. |
| **اللهم إني أصبحت أشهدك… ×4** (cited Abu Dawud 5078) | **(memory)** Weak per Albani in Abu Dawud; the app also marks it weak. The Abu Dawud number should be checked (5069 in the usual numbering). |
| **أصبحنا وأصبح الملك لله رب العالمين، أسألك خير هذا اليوم** (Abu Dawud 5084) | **(memory)** Weak per Albani (the app already marks it weak). Appears twice in the morning folder (cards 19 and 34). |
| **اللهم صل وسلم على نبينا ×100** (Tabarani, "عشرًا صباحًا وعشرًا مساءً") | **(memory)** The reward hadith is weak (Albani, Silsila Da'ifa 5788). The count in the app (100) does not match the hadith (**10**). |
| **اللهم أنت ربي لا إله إلا أنت عليك توكلت… (Ibn al-Sunni)** | **(memory)** Weak (Albani, Al-Kalim al-Tayyib). |
| **ابن السني: أصبحنا وأصبح الملك لله عز وجل / الحمد لله الذي جللنا اليوم عافيته** | **(memory)** Weak or unverified chains (the app itself gives no grade or "weak"). |
| **أصبحنا على فطرة الإسلام (ابن السني 33)** — duplicate | **(memory)** Duplicate of the authentic Ahmad version (card 13) — keep one. |
| **حسبي الله لا إله إلا هو ×7** | **(web)** Mixed: the **marfu'** (to the Prophet ﷺ) version is weak, but the version **stopping at Abu al-Darda'** is authentic in Abu Dawud (Ibn Baz: *jayyid chain, and such a statement carries the ruling of marfu'*). The extra "من أمر الدنيا والآخرة" is not in Abu Dawud. **Valid to keep**, but the card should say it is *mawquf* on Abu al-Darda'. |
